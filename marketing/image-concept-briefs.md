# Review Jam Image Concept Briefs

Six static image assets for marketing, social media, and pitch materials.

---

## 1. "The Missing Middle"

**Purpose:** Show that Review Jam occupies the honest center between paid praise and rage reviews.

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 628 px (optimized for social sharing, LinkedIn, Twitter/X)

**Layout Description:**
Three equal-width columns side by side, separated by thin vertical dividers. Each column contains a stylized review card mockup.

- Left column: A fake 5-star review card. The card has a golden/yellow background tint. The review text is generic praise ("Best product ever! 10/10 would recommend!"). A red diagonal "PAID" stamp overlays the card. The star rating shows 5 filled stars. Below the card, a label reads "Paid Review." The overall feel is glossy and untrustworthy.

- Center column: A Review Jam review card. The card has a clean white background with a subtle purple border. The review text is balanced and specific ("Solid build quality. Battery lasts 6 hours, not the advertised 8. Camera is excellent in daylight, average in low light. Worth it at $599."). A Health Score badge shows "87/100" in a circular gauge. A green "Receipt Verified" checkmark appears in the top-right corner. Below the card, a label reads "Review Jam Review" in purple. The overall feel is clean, credible, and detailed.

- Right column: An angry 1-star review card. The card has a red background tint. The review text is all caps and emotional ("WORST PURCHASE OF MY LIFE. DO NOT BUY. TOTAL SCAM!!!"). The star rating shows 1 filled star. A red diagonal "RAGE" stamp overlays the card. Below the card, a label reads "Rage Review." The overall feel is hostile and unhelpful.

Above the three columns, a headline reads: "Which review would you trust?"

Below the three columns, the Review Jam logo and tagline: "Own the Purchase Decision Layer."

**Color Palette:**
- Left column accent: #FFD700 (gold) with #CC0000 (red) stamp
- Center column accent: #7C3AED (Review Jam purple) with #10B981 (green) verification badge
- Right column accent: #DC2626 (red) with #CC0000 (red) stamp
- Background: #F8FAFC (light gray)
- Text: #1E293B (dark slate)
- Headline: #0F172A (near black)

**Text Content:**
- Headline: "Which review would you trust?"
- Left label: "Paid Review"
- Center label: "Review Jam Review"
- Right label: "Rage Review"
- Footer: Review Jam logo + "Own the Purchase Decision Layer."

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- The center column should be slightly elevated or have a subtle drop shadow to draw the eye
- The left and right columns should feel slightly faded or muted compared to the center
- The "PAID" and "RAGE" stamps should be tilted at approximately 15 degrees
- The Health Score badge in the center should be the most visually prominent element
- Use a clean sans-serif font throughout (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or similar)
- Ensure the review text in the center column is legible at the target dimensions
- The overall composition should immediately communicate "the middle option is the right one" without needing to read the text

## 2. "Cold Start Solution"

**Purpose:** Show how Review Jam solves the zero-review problem for new product launches.

**Dimensions:** 1080 x 1080 px (optimized for Instagram, square social)

**Layout Description:**
A before/after split layout, divided vertically down the center.

- Left half (Before): A product page mockup in a desaturated, gray-toned style. The product image is a generic electronics product (headphones, phone, or similar). Below the product image, the area where reviews would appear shows: "0 Reviews" in gray text, an empty star rating row (all 5 stars are hollow/unfilled), and a message: "Be the first to review this product." The overall feel is empty, cold, and uninviting. A "BEFORE" label appears at the top of this half. A faint red "X" or prohibition sign overlays this section.

- Right half (After): The same product page mockup, now in full color with vibrant tones. Below the product image, the review area shows: "24 Verified Reviews" in bold text, a Health Score of "82/100" in a circular badge, three preview review cards stacked with visible Health Scores (91, 85, 78), each with a green "Receipt Verified" checkmark. The overall feel is alive, trusted, and inviting. An "AFTER" label appears at the top of this half. A subtle green glow or highlight frames this section.

Between the two halves, a circular arrow or transformation icon indicates the change. Below the arrow: "Review Jam Bounty Pool: $500 funded, 24 reviews in 5 days."

At the bottom, the Review Jam logo and text: "Solve cold start in days, not months."

**Color Palette:**
- Left half: #94A3B8 (slate gray), #E2E8F0 (light gray), desaturated tones throughout
- Right half: #7C3AED (purple accent), #10B981 (green verification), #FFFFFF (white cards), full vibrant colors
- Transformation arrow: #7C3AED (purple) to #10B981 (green) gradient
- Background: #FFFFFF (white)
- Text: #1E293B (dark slate)

**Text Content:**
- Left header: "BEFORE"
- Left review area: "0 Reviews" / "Be the first to review this product."
- Right header: "AFTER"
- Right review area: "24 Verified Reviews" / Health Score "82/100"
- Center callout: "Review Jam Bounty Pool: $500 funded, 24 reviews in 5 days"
- Footer: "Solve cold start in days, not months."

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- The contrast between the two halves should be stark and immediate
- The left half should feel genuinely empty and unappealing, like a product page nobody has visited
- The right half should feel bustling with activity and credibility
- The three stacked review cards on the right should show just enough text to look real (2 lines each) but the focus is on the Health Score badges and verification checkmarks
- Consider adding small avatar circles next to each review card to show real people
- The transformation element in the center should draw the eye and create a clear cause-effect narrative
- Test legibility at both 1080px (full size) and 540px (mobile half-size)

## 3. "Purchase Decision Funnel"

**Purpose:** Visualize the marketing funnel with Review Jam owning the critical final stage before purchase.

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 675 px (optimized for presentation slides and LinkedIn)

**Layout Description:**
A vertical funnel diagram oriented from top to bottom, taking up the left two-thirds of the image. The right third contains supporting text.

The funnel has four stages, each progressively narrower:

- Stage 1 (widest, top): "Advertising & Social Media." Background color is a neutral blue-gray. Icons of ad platforms (generic ad icon, social media icons) float around this stage. Dollar signs ($$$) rain into the top of the funnel. Label to the right: "$600B+ spent annually."

- Stage 2 (narrower): "Awareness & Interest." Background color is a slightly warmer blue. Icons of eyeballs and click cursors. Label: "Consumers discover the product."

- Stage 3 (narrower still): "The Purchase Decision Layer." This stage is highlighted with a glowing border in Review Jam purple. The Review Jam logo sits inside this stage. Icons of review cards, Health Score badges, and verification checkmarks appear here. This is the visual focal point of the entire image. Label: "Reviews close the sale. Review Jam owns this layer."

- Stage 4 (narrowest, bottom): "Purchase." A shopping cart or "Buy Now" button icon. A green checkmark. Label: "Conversion."

An arrow exits the bottom of the funnel into a dollar sign with a circular motion indicating revenue.

Below the funnel: "Ads don't convince customers to buy. Reviews do."

**Color Palette:**
- Stage 1: #64748B (slate) with #94A3B8 (light slate)
- Stage 2: #6366F1 (indigo) with #818CF8 (light indigo)
- Stage 3: #7C3AED (Review Jam purple) with #A78BFA (light purple), glowing border effect
- Stage 4: #10B981 (green) with #34D399 (light green)
- Dollar signs: #F59E0B (amber)
- Background: #0F172A (dark navy) for contrast
- Text: #F8FAFC (white/near-white)

**Text Content:**
- Stage 1: "Advertising & Social Media" / "$600B+ spent annually"
- Stage 2: "Awareness & Interest" / "Consumers discover the product"
- Stage 3: "The Purchase Decision Layer" / "Reviews close the sale"
- Stage 4: "Purchase" / "Conversion"
- Footer: "Ads don't convince customers to buy. Reviews do."
- Review Jam logo positioned inside Stage 3

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- Stage 3 (Purchase Decision Layer) must be the unmistakable visual hero of the image. Use a glowing border, larger size relative to the other stages, or a spotlight effect
- The dark background creates contrast that makes the purple glow of Stage 3 pop
- Dollar signs entering the top and the conversion at the bottom should create a clear flow narrative
- The funnel should feel like a natural, familiar marketing diagram that the viewer instantly understands
- Avoid making the funnel look like a generic stock image. The Review Jam branding inside Stage 3 should feel integral, not pasted on
- Consider a subtle animation-ready composition (if this asset is later animated, stages could fill sequentially from top to bottom)

## 4. "Health Score Explainer"

**Purpose:** Break down the Health Score into its four component pillars with example scoring.

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 900 px (optimized for blog posts, landing pages, and pitch decks)

**Layout Description:**
Top section: A large circular Health Score gauge showing "87/100" as the composite score. The gauge is a thick circular arc, mostly filled in purple, with the number "87" in large bold text in the center and "/100" in smaller text below it. A label above the gauge reads "Health Score."

Below the main gauge, four equal-width pillar cards are arranged horizontally:

- Pillar 1: "Quality" card. A smaller circular gauge showing "92." Icon: a magnifying glass or checklist. Below the gauge: "Depth of analysis, specific details, helpful comparisons, clear writing." Background tint: blue.

- Pillar 2: "Engagement" card. A smaller circular gauge showing "85." Icon: a chat bubble or hands clapping. Below the gauge: "Upvotes, replies, bookmarks, community interaction, time spent reading." Background tint: green.

- Pillar 3: "Credibility" card. A smaller circular gauge showing "90." Icon: a shield with a checkmark. Below the gauge: "Receipt verification, review history, trust tier, consistency across reviews." Background tint: purple.

- Pillar 4: "Freshness" card. A smaller circular gauge showing "78." Icon: a clock or calendar. Below the gauge: "Recency of review, Ownership Journey updates, relevance to current product version." Background tint: orange.

Below the four pillars, a short callout bar reads: "Health Score rewards quality and honesty, not star ratings. A thorough 2-star review scores higher than a lazy 5-star review."

At the bottom, the Review Jam logo.

**Color Palette:**
- Main gauge: #7C3AED (purple) arc on #E2E8F0 (light gray) track
- Quality pillar: #3B82F6 (blue) accent
- Engagement pillar: #10B981 (green) accent
- Credibility pillar: #7C3AED (purple) accent
- Freshness pillar: #F59E0B (amber/orange) accent
- Card backgrounds: white with the pillar accent color as a subtle top border or left border
- Overall background: #F8FAFC (light gray)
- Text: #1E293B (dark slate)
- Callout bar: #7C3AED (purple) background with white text

**Text Content:**
- Main header: "Health Score"
- Main gauge: "87 / 100"
- Pillar 1: "Quality" / "92" / description text
- Pillar 2: "Engagement" / "85" / description text
- Pillar 3: "Credibility" / "90" / description text
- Pillar 4: "Freshness" / "78" / description text
- Callout: "Health Score rewards quality and honesty, not star ratings."
- Footer: Review Jam logo

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- The main Health Score gauge should be large enough to be immediately readable (at least 150px diameter in the final render)
- Each pillar gauge should be smaller but still clearly legible (approximately 80px diameter)
- The four pillar cards should have equal visual weight. No one pillar should dominate
- Use the pillar accent colors consistently: the gauge arc, the icon, and any highlighting should all use the same color per pillar
- The composition should read from top (composite score) to bottom (component pillars), creating a clear "this is how the score is calculated" narrative
- Add subtle connecting lines or arrows from the four pillars up to the main gauge to visually reinforce that the pillars compose the total score
- Ensure all gauge numbers are in a bold, highly legible typeface

## 5. "Earnings Showcase"

**Purpose:** Show a concrete example of a reviewer earning money from a single review.

**Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350 px (optimized for Instagram Story, portrait social)

**Layout Description:**
The image is composed of two overlapping layers:

- Background layer: A review card in the Review Jam UI style. The card shows:
  - Product name: "Sony WH-1000XM5 Headphones"
  - Star rating: 4 out of 5 stars filled
  - Health Score badge: "91/100" in a circular gauge (top-right corner of the card)
  - Reviewer name and avatar: "Alex M." with a small circular avatar placeholder. Trust tier badge: "Trusted" (tier 3 of 5, shown as a small shield icon)
  - "Receipt Verified" green checkmark
  - Review excerpt (3-4 lines): "Outstanding noise cancellation. Comfort is excellent for all-day wear. Call quality is noticeably better than the XM4. Battery life hits the advertised 30 hours. One issue: the touch controls are overly sensitive and I accidentally skip tracks constantly. At $349, this is the best ANC headphone available if you can tolerate the touch controls."
  - Engagement metrics row at the bottom: "47 upvotes / 12 replies / 8 bookmarks"
  - Ownership Journey indicator: "Updated at 3 months" with a small timeline showing checkpoints

- Overlay layer: A wallet/earnings card that overlaps the bottom-right corner of the review card. The earnings card is smaller, angled slightly (5 degrees clockwise tilt), and has a green-to-purple gradient background. It contains:
  - A dollar sign icon at the top
  - "$12.50 earned" in large, bold white text
  - "from Sony WH-1000XM5 bounty pool" in smaller text below
  - "Health Score: 91 (top 8% of reviews)" in even smaller text
  - A subtle "Paid" checkmark at the bottom

At the very top of the image: "Your honest review has value."

At the very bottom: Review Jam logo and "Get paid for what you already do."

**Color Palette:**
- Review card: #FFFFFF (white) with #E2E8F0 (light gray) border
- Health Score gauge: #7C3AED (purple) arc
- Verification badge: #10B981 (green)
- Star rating: #F59E0B (amber)
- Earnings overlay gradient: #10B981 (green) to #7C3AED (purple)
- Earnings text: #FFFFFF (white)
- Background behind the card: #F1F5F9 (soft gray) or a subtle gradient
- Text: #1E293B (dark slate) on the review card

**Text Content:**
- Top header: "Your honest review has value."
- Product: "Sony WH-1000XM5 Headphones"
- Health Score: "91/100"
- Reviewer: "Alex M." / "Trusted"
- Review text: The excerpt described above
- Engagement: "47 upvotes / 12 replies / 8 bookmarks"
- Earnings overlay: "$12.50 earned" / "from Sony WH-1000XM5 bounty pool"
- Footer: "Get paid for what you already do."

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- The review card should look like a realistic screenshot of the actual Review Jam UI. If the product UI is available, match the design system exactly
- The earnings overlay should feel like a notification or popup that appeared on top of the review, creating a "look what you earned" moment
- The tilt on the earnings overlay (5 degrees) should feel playful but not chaotic
- The $12.50 amount should be the single most eye-catching element on the entire image
- The review text should be long enough to look thorough and real, but the focus is on the earnings overlay, not the text
- Consider a subtle green glow or highlight around the earnings overlay to draw attention
- The overall composition should communicate: "This is a real review. This person earned real money. This could be you."
- Make sure the image works both at full resolution and as a thumbnail (the $12.50 should be legible even when small)

## 6. "Trust Tier Progression"

**Purpose:** Visualize the reviewer reputation journey from Newcomer to Legend.

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 628 px (optimized for social sharing, Twitter/X cards, LinkedIn)

**Layout Description:**
A horizontal progression path running from left to right across the image. The path is styled as a road, trail, or series of connected stepping stones.

Five stations along the path, each representing a trust tier:

- Station 1 (far left): "Newcomer." Icon: a small seedling or sprout. Badge is a simple gray circle. Description below: "Just getting started. Write your first verified review." The area around this station is minimal and sparse.

- Station 2: "Contributor." Icon: a small plant with a few leaves. Badge is a bronze/copper circle. Description: "5+ reviews, consistent quality. Building your voice." The area is slightly more developed.

- Station 3 (center): "Trusted." Icon: a healthy plant or small tree. Badge is a silver circle with a subtle glow. Description: "20+ reviews, high Health Scores. The community relies on you." The area shows more growth and activity.

- Station 4: "Authority." Icon: a large tree with full canopy. Badge is a gold circle with a brighter glow. Description: "50+ reviews, top 10% Health Scores. Brands seek your opinion." The area is lush and prominent.

- Station 5 (far right): "Legend." Icon: a majestic tree with a crown or halo effect. Badge is a purple/diamond circle with a strong glow and subtle particles. Description: "100+ reviews, consistently exceptional. The pinnacle of reviewer trust." The area is the most visually impressive, with a subtle golden light or spotlight effect.

The path connecting the stations has small markers or footsteps to show progress. A dotted-line arrow runs along the top of the path from left to right, labeled "Your Journey."

Below the path, a horizontal bar shows the aggregate benefits increasing with each tier: "Earnings multiplier," "Community influence," "Brand partnership opportunities."

At the bottom: Review Jam logo and "Your reputation grows with every honest review."

**Color Palette:**
- Newcomer: #94A3B8 (gray)
- Contributor: #D97706 (bronze/copper)
- Trusted: #A1A1AA (silver) with #E2E8F0 glow
- Authority: #F59E0B (gold) with #FDE68A glow
- Legend: #7C3AED (purple) with #C4B5FD glow and #F59E0B (gold) particle accents
- Path/road: #E2E8F0 (light gray) to #CBD5E1 (medium gray)
- Background: Linear gradient from #F8FAFC (left, light) to #1E1B4B (right, dark purple/navy), reflecting the journey from beginner to elite
- Text: #1E293B (dark) on light backgrounds, #F8FAFC (white) on dark backgrounds
- Arrow: #7C3AED (purple) dashed line

**Text Content:**
- Path label: "Your Journey"
- Station 1: "Newcomer" / "Just getting started"
- Station 2: "Contributor" / "Building your voice"
- Station 3: "Trusted" / "The community relies on you"
- Station 4: "Authority" / "Brands seek your opinion"
- Station 5: "Legend" / "The pinnacle of reviewer trust"
- Benefits bar: "Earnings multiplier / Community influence / Brand partnerships"
- Footer: "Your reputation grows with every honest review."

**Design Notes for Designer:**
- The progression from left to right should feel like a genuine journey with increasing visual richness
- The background gradient (light to dark to dramatic) reinforces the sense of elevation and achievement
- Each tier badge should be clearly distinct from the others in color and complexity
- The Legend tier should feel genuinely aspirational and impressive without being gaudy
- The plant/tree growth metaphor (seedling to majestic tree) works well but is optional. An alternative could be abstract geometric shapes that increase in complexity, or a mountain ascent
- The benefits bar at the bottom should use small icons or a visual scale to show that benefits increase at each tier
- Ensure the five tier names are all legible at the target dimensions. They are the primary information the viewer needs to extract
- Consider how this image could be cropped to 1:1 for Instagram. The center three tiers (Contributor, Trusted, Authority) should still tell a coherent story if the edges are cropped
- The overall tone should be motivational: "You are on a journey. There is always a next level."
