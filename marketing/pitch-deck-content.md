## Slide 2: The Problem

**Headline:** Reviews Are Broken

**Bullet Points:**
- Paid reviews: Brands buy 5-star sentiment, eroding consumer trust
- Rage reviews: Emotional 1-star dumps with no nuance or context
- Missing reviews: New products launch with zero social proof (the cold start problem)
- 93% of consumers say reviews influence their purchase decisions, yet the ecosystem rewards manipulation over honesty
- Result: a $50B+ review economy that nobody trusts

**Speaker Notes:**
Walk through each failure mode. Paid reviews (Amazon Vine abuse, fake review farms) have trained consumers to distrust anything that looks too positive. Rage reviews flood platforms after viral incidents but offer no balanced perspective. And the biggest gap: new products with genuinely zero reviews, where consumers simply will not buy. The review ecosystem is broken at every level, and trust is the casualty.

Right column: a blank product page showing "0 reviews" with a "MISSING" stamp. All three columns have a red X beneath them. The layout should feel like a visual diagnosis.

**Bullet Points:**

**Speaker Notes:**

The AI bias check runs silently in the background. And earnings are automatic, paid out from bounty pools based on how helpful your review is to the community, not based on whether you gave 5 stars or 1 star.

## Slide 9: Business Model

**Headline:** Brand-Funded Bounty Pools, Engagement-Weighted Payouts

**Bullet Points:**
- Revenue source: Brands fund bounty pools for their products (minimum pool sizes by tier)
- Payout mechanism: Pool funds are distributed to reviewers proportional to their review's Health Score
- Critical differentiator: Brand funding is completely decoupled from review sentiment. Brands pay for reviews to exist, not for reviews to be positive
- Platform take rate: Review Jam retains a percentage of each bounty pool as platform revenue
- Additional revenue streams: Premium brand analytics, widget customization, priority placement, API access
- Flywheel: More reviews attract more consumers. More consumers attract more brands. More brands fund more bounty pools. More bounty pools attract more reviewers.

**Speaker Notes:**
The business model is the heart of why Review Jam works. The key insight is the decoupling of funding from sentiment. On traditional platforms, brands that pay for reviews expect positive sentiment, which corrupts the entire ecosystem. On Review Jam, brands fund a bounty pool and then the community writes whatever they honestly think. A brand cannot pull funding because of a negative review. They cannot target specific reviewers. They can only fund the pool and let the ecosystem work. This is what makes the reviews trustworthy, which is what makes the reviews valuable, which is what makes brands willing to fund the pools. Walk through the flywheel to show how this creates a self-reinforcing growth loop.

**Suggested Visual:**
Left side: a diagram showing money flowing from a "Brand" icon into a "Bounty Pool" bucket, then distributing outward to multiple "Reviewer" icons with varying payout amounts (correlated to Health Score badges next to each reviewer). Right side: a circular flywheel diagram with four nodes: More Reviews, More Consumers, More Brands, More Bounty Pools, with arrows connecting them in a cycle. Use green for money flows and purple for the flywheel.

## Slide 10: Competitive Landscape

**Headline:** Review Jam vs. The Status Quo

**Bullet Points:**
- Amazon Reviews: Massive scale but rampant manipulation, no reviewer compensation, no verified ownership journey, incentivized positive bias through Vine program
- Trustpilot: Business-focused but pay-to-play perception, limited consumer trust, no bounty model, businesses can game rankings
- G2 / Capterra: B2B software only, gift-card incentivized, no ownership verification, narrow vertical
- Influencer Reviews (YouTube, TikTok): Engaging but undisclosed sponsorships, no structured quality scoring, entertainment over accuracy, no long-term updates
- Review Jam: Receipt-verified ownership, sentiment-independent payouts, algorithmic Health Score, Ownership Journey updates, structured counter-takes, trust tier reputation system

**Speaker Notes:**
Do not trash competitors. Instead, frame the landscape as each existing solution having a structural limitation that Review Jam addresses. Amazon has scale but no trust. Trustpilot has business relationships but consumers do not trust it. G2 is vertical-specific. Influencers are entertaining but not systematic. Review Jam is the first platform where the economic incentives, verification systems, and quality algorithms are all aligned toward honest, comprehensive reviews. The positioning is not "we are better" but "we are solving a different problem that none of them can solve because of their structural incentives."

**Suggested Visual:**
A comparison matrix table. Rows: Verified Ownership, Sentiment-Independent Pay, Health Score, Ownership Journey, Review Forking, Brand Bounty Pools. Columns: Amazon, Trustpilot, G2, Influencers, Review Jam. Checkmarks and X marks in each cell. Review Jam column is fully green with all checkmarks. Other columns have a mix.
