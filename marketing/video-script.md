# Review Jam -- Hero Video Script

**Format:** 60-90 second brand hero video
**Tone:** Confident, modern, slightly irreverent. Not salesy -- factual and sharp.
**Visual Style:** Clean motion graphics on dark background (deep navy / near-black), punctuated by bright UI reveals on white. High contrast transitions. No stock footage of people smiling at laptops. Typography-driven with product UI as the star.
**Music:** Minimal electronic build -- tension in the first half, resolving into a steady confident beat at the midpoint. Think Atticus Ross, not ukulele.
**Aspect Ratios:** Deliver 16:9 (primary), 9:16 (social cutdown), 1:1 (feed)

---

## SEGMENT 1 -- THE HOOK

**Duration:** 0:00 - 0:08

### Shot 1A (0:00 - 0:04)

**On Screen:** Dark background. A stylized phone screen appears center-frame. A flashy product ad auto-plays on the phone -- bright colors, bold claims, a new wireless earbud or skincare product. The ad is deliberately over-produced and generic.

**Text Overlay:** (appears letter-by-letter, large white sans-serif, caps)
`YOU SAW AN AD.`

**Audio:** Low bass hum. Muted ad audio bleeds through, tinny and distant.

**Visual Notes:** The ad content should feel familiar and disposable. Use motion blur on the ad itself -- it's not the point. The phone floats with a subtle parallax drift.

### Shot 1B (0:04 - 0:08)

**On Screen:** The ad shrinks to the top-left corner. The user's thumb appears and taps away from the ad. A browser search bar animates in. The thumb types: `"[product name] review"`. Search results populate.

**Text Overlay:**
`WHAT DO YOU DO NEXT?`

**Audio:** A single clean keystroke sound per letter typed. Bass hum continues.

**Visual Notes:** The typing animation should feel real -- slight hesitation, natural rhythm. This is the universal behavior. Everyone does this.

---

## SEGMENT 2 -- THE PROBLEM

**Duration:** 0:08 - 0:22

### Shot 2A (0:08 - 0:14)

**On Screen:** Cut to a product listing page (stylized, not a real brand). The star rating dominates: a large, glowing **4.8 stars** with **"10,247 reviews"** underneath. The page looks polished and trustworthy at first glance.

Camera slowly zooms into the review section. Reviews scroll by:

- "Amazing product! 5 stars!" (posted 2 days ago)
- "Best purchase ever!!!" (posted 2 days ago)
- "Changed my life. 5/5" (posted 3 days ago)
- "Love it so much!!!!" (posted 3 days ago)

They all look the same. Same cadence, same enthusiasm, same emptiness.

**Text Overlay:** (sharp cut-in, centered)
`4.8 STARS. 10,000 REVIEWS.`

**Audio:** Music adds a tense mid-range synth layer. Subtle dissonance.

**Visual Notes:** The reviews should feel uncanny -- not obviously fake, but suspiciously uniform. Use a slight desaturation or color shift to put the viewer on edge. The scroll speed accelerates gradually.

### Shot 2B (0:14 - 0:18)

**On Screen:** The review feed freezes. A red translucent overlay washes across the entire review section. One by one, review cards start flagging themselves: a small icon appears on each -- a warning triangle, a "likely incentivized" tag, a "no verified purchase" badge.

**Text Overlay:** (bold, punchy)
`YOU TRUST NONE OF THEM.`

**Audio:** A low percussive hit on the word "NONE." Music drops to just bass.

**Visual Notes:** The flagging animation should feel like the system is catching things the human eye missed. Each flag appears with a subtle pulse. Not aggressive -- clinical.

### Shot 2C (0:18 - 0:22)

**On Screen:** Quick montage -- three rapid cuts (1.3 seconds each):
1. A fake 5-star review with a receipt icon crossed out (no proof of purchase)
2. A 1-star rage review with ALL CAPS and exclamation marks ("WORST PRODUCT EVER!!!!")
3. A brand new product page with a completely empty review section -- "0 reviews. Be the first!"

**Text Overlay:** (staggered, one line per cut)
`PAID PRAISE.`
`RAGE REVIEWS.`
`RADIO SILENCE.`

**Audio:** Three sharp staccato hits, one per cut. Tight and rhythmic.

**Visual Notes:** Each card should feel like a different failure mode. Use slight camera shake on each cut. The empty review state should feel especially bleak -- a good product with zero social proof.

---

## SEGMENT 3 -- THE INSIGHT

**Duration:** 0:22 - 0:30

### Shot 3A (0:22 - 0:26)

**On Screen:** Black screen. A simple animated diagram fades in:

```
AD -----> AWARENESS
              |
              v
         [BROKEN LINK]
              |
              v
REVIEW -----> PURCHASE
```

The chain from AD to AWARENESS is bright and connected. The link between AWARENESS and PURCHASE is visually broken -- a cracked line, a gap, static.

**Text Overlay:**
`ADS CREATE AWARENESS.`

**Audio:** Music is sparse. Just a pulsing low tone.

**Visual Notes:** The diagram should feel like a system schematic -- technical, clean, almost like an engineering blueprint on a dark background.

### Shot 3B (0:26 - 0:30)

**On Screen:** The broken link in the diagram highlights and pulses red. Then the word "REVIEWS" animates into the gap, bridging the connection. The full chain lights up green for a moment.

**Text Overlay:**
`REVIEWS CLOSE THE SALE.`
(beat)
`REVIEWS ARE BROKEN.`

**Audio:** The green moment gets a brief harmonic resolve. Then a hard cut to silence on "BROKEN."

**Visual Notes:** The transition from connected-green to the word "BROKEN" should feel like a gut punch. One second of hope, then reality.

---

## SEGMENT 4 -- THE SOLUTION

**Duration:** 0:30 - 0:50

### Shot 4A (0:30 - 0:35)

**On Screen:** Hard cut to the Review Jam logo on a clean white background. The logo lands with weight -- a subtle bounce or scale-in. Below it, the tagline fades in:

`The trust layer for purchase decisions.`

Then the background transitions from white to the Review Jam UI -- a product page on the platform.

**Text Overlay:**
`REVIEW JAM`

**Audio:** Music shifts. The tension breaks into a confident, forward-moving beat. Clean, modern, energetic but not frantic.

**Visual Notes:** This is the tonal pivot. Everything before was dark and skeptical. Now we're in a bright, clean, trustworthy product world. The UI should feel premium -- generous whitespace, clear typography, intentional color.

### Shot 4B (0:35 - 0:42)

**On Screen:** The Review Jam product page is on screen. Camera focuses on three features, each getting a 2-3 second spotlight:

**Feature 1 -- Health Score:**
A circular gauge animates from 0 to 82. The number is large and confident. Below it: "Based on depth, verification, and balance." Color shifts from red (low) through amber to green (high) as it climbs.

**Feature 2 -- Bias Detection:**
A review card slides in. An AI annotation appears inline: "This review is flagged as disproportionately positive relative to verified peer reviews." The flag is matter-of-fact, not accusatory. A small "Bias Analysis" badge pulses.

**Feature 3 -- Verified Purchase:**
A receipt icon animates in. A phone camera viewfinder appears, scanning a receipt. A green checkmark stamps onto the review card: "Purchase Verified via OCR."

**Text Overlay:** (one per feature, bottom-left, small caps)
`HEALTH SCORE: 0-100`
`AI BIAS DETECTION`
`RECEIPT-VERIFIED PURCHASES`

**Audio:** Each feature transition gets a soft chime or click. Music maintains momentum.

**Visual Notes:** Each feature reveal should feel like peeling back a layer. The UI is the hero -- not abstract graphics. Show the actual product interface. Transitions between features: smooth horizontal wipes.

### Shot 4C (0:42 - 0:50)

**On Screen:** Pull back to show the full Review Jam product page with all three features visible. Then, secondary feature badges animate in around the main review:

- **Ownership Journey:** A timeline appears below the review -- "1 month," "3 months," "6 months," "1 year" -- with the reviewer's updated ratings at each checkpoint. The review evolves over time.
- **Review Forking:** A "Counter-Take" button pulses. A second reviewer's card slides in alongside the original -- a respectful disagreement, side by side.
- **Trust Tier:** A small badge on the reviewer's avatar: "Verified -- Trusted" with a tier icon (e.g., a small shield).

**Text Overlay:**
`REVIEWS THAT EVOLVE. PERSPECTIVES THAT COMPETE.`
`TRUST THAT'S EARNED.`

**Audio:** Music builds slightly. Layered now -- more instruments, more confidence.

**Visual Notes:** This shot should feel like the product breathing -- alive, dynamic, multi-dimensional. The Ownership Journey timeline is the standout visual. Show the rating actually changing over time (e.g., 4 stars at 1 month, 3 stars at 6 months) to demonstrate honesty.

---

## SEGMENT 5 -- HOW IT WORKS (REVIEWER FLOW)

**Duration:** 0:50 - 1:05

### Shot 5A (0:50 - 0:55)

**On Screen:** Scene shift. A clean, minimal animation of the reviewer flow:

Step 1: A hand icon writes a review. Text appears in a review card -- real sentences, not lorem ipsum. Something like: "The battery life is solid for the first two weeks, but I noticed a 15% drop by month three. The noise cancellation is best-in-class for the price."

**Text Overlay:**
`WRITE AN HONEST REVIEW.`

**Audio:** Music continues at a steady, warm pace.

**Visual Notes:** The review text should feel specific, balanced, and real. Not gushing, not complaining. Just a person being useful. The writing animation should have a human pace.

### Shot 5B (0:55 - 1:00)

**On Screen:** The review card gets scored. The Health Score gauge appears and fills to 88. Breakdown tags animate in: "Depth: High," "Specificity: High," "Verified: Yes," "Sentiment Bias: Low."

A trust tier badge upgrades: the reviewer's profile shifts from "Newcomer" to "Rising" with a subtle glow.

**Text Overlay:**
`GET SCORED ON QUALITY. NOT SENTIMENT.`

**Audio:** A satisfying "level up" sound on the tier upgrade. Musical.

**Visual Notes:** The scoring should feel meritocratic and transparent. The viewer should understand immediately: good reviews are specific, honest, and verified. The system rewards substance.

### Shot 5C (1:00 - 1:05)

**On Screen:** The review card is now live. An engagement counter ticks up -- views, "helpful" votes, saves. A small earnings indicator appears in the corner of the reviewer's dashboard: "$4.20 earned" ticking up in real time as engagement grows.

The earnings are modest and honest -- not "GET RICH REVIEWING." Just: your work has value.

**Text Overlay:**
`EARN FROM ENGAGEMENT. NOT FROM STARS.`

**Audio:** The ticking counter has a soft, rhythmic click. Satisfying.

**Visual Notes:** Keep the earnings grounded. This is not a get-rich-quick pitch. Show a realistic dashboard with modest but real numbers. The counter should feel like a coffee-drip of value -- steady and earned.

---

## SEGMENT 6 -- THE THREE AUDIENCES

**Duration:** 1:05 - 1:15

### Shot 6 (1:05 - 1:15)

**On Screen:** Screen splits into three vertical panels, each highlighting a stakeholder:

**Panel 1 -- Brands (left):**
A new product with zero reviews. The Review Jam widget appears on the product page. Reviews start populating. The "cold start" problem dissolves. Text: "Solve the cold start problem."

**Panel 2 -- Reviewers (center, slightly larger):**
A reviewer's profile dashboard. Trust tier: "Trusted." Lifetime earnings visible. A queue of products awaiting honest review. Text: "Get paid for what you already do."

**Panel 3 -- Consumers (right):**
A shopper scrolling Review Jam. Health Scores visible on every product. They tap a review, see the Ownership Journey, see the bias flags. They make a purchase with confidence. Text: "Buy with real confidence."

**Text Overlay:** (appears below all three panels)
`ONE PLATFORM. THREE WINS.`

**Audio:** Music reaches its highest energy here. All layers playing.

**Visual Notes:** The three-panel layout should feel balanced but the reviewer panel is hero-positioned (center, 10% wider). Each panel animates simultaneously but with slight stagger (left, center, right, 200ms apart). Clean divider lines between panels.

---

## SEGMENT 7 -- CTA

**Duration:** 1:15 - 1:25

### Shot 7A (1:15 - 1:20)

**On Screen:** The three panels collapse back to a single frame. Dark background returns. Center frame, large typography:

`YOUR HONEST OPINION HAS VALUE.`

Beat.

`LITERALLY.`

The word "LITERALLY" lands with a subtle scale punch -- 5% larger, then settles.

**Audio:** Music resolves to a final chord. Clean, confident, done.

**Visual Notes:** This is the line people should remember. The pause between the two lines is critical -- 0.8 seconds of silence. "LITERALLY" should feel like the mic drop.

### Shot 7B (1:20 - 1:25)

**On Screen:** Review Jam logo fades in, centered. Below it:

`reviewjam.com`
`Join the waitlist.`

Small text at the bottom: "For reviewers, brands, and people who read reviews before buying anything."

**Audio:** Music out. A single soft tone rings and fades -- like a bell at the end of a meditation. Clean silence.

**Visual Notes:** The end card should breathe. No clutter. Logo, URL, one CTA, one line of personality. Hold for 4 seconds minimum. The small text at the bottom is the wink -- it should feel like an inside joke for the target audience.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Typography
- **Primary font:** Inter or Satoshi -- geometric, modern, high legibility
- **Display font:** For "LITERALLY" and key impact lines, consider a tighter display weight or a custom letterpress treatment
- **All text overlays:** Left-aligned or center-aligned, never right-aligned. Generous leading. Text should never compete with UI elements on screen.

### Color Palette
- **Dark segments (1-3, 7):** Background #0A0A0F (near-black with blue undertone), text #FFFFFF, accent #FF4444 (for warnings/flags)
- **Light segments (4-6):** Background #FAFAFA, text #111111, accent #22C55E (Health Score green), secondary accent #3B82F6 (Review Jam blue)
- **Transitions:** Dark-to-light transitions should feel like a curtain opening. Use a horizontal wipe or a radial reveal from center.

### Motion Principles
- **Easing:** Use ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits. Never linear.
- **Duration:** UI element animations between 300-500ms. Text overlays appear over 200ms.
- **Parallax:** Subtle depth on all phone/device mockups. 2-3% movement on scroll or camera drift.
- **No bounce physics** on UI elements -- this is a trust product, not a toy.

### Pacing
- Segments 1-3 (Hook through Insight): Faster cuts, higher tension, 1-3 seconds per shot
- Segments 4-6 (Solution through Audiences): Slower, more breathing room, 3-5 seconds per feature
- Segment 7 (CTA): Spacious. Let the final line land.

### Audio Direction
- **Music:** Commission or license a minimal electronic track. Reference: Atticus Ross film scores, Tycho's more restrained work. No vocals. Build from sparse to full across the 75-second runtime.
- **SFX:** All UI sounds should be custom and subtle -- soft clicks, chimes, and tones. Nothing cartoonish. Reference: Apple product videos, Stripe's brand films.
- **No voiceover.** This video is text-and-music driven. The viewer reads. This creates focus and feels more modern. (If a VO version is needed for accessibility or ads, record separately as an alternate cut.)

### Deliverables
| Asset | Specs |
|---|---|
| Hero video (primary) | 16:9, 1920x1080, 60fps, H.264 + ProRes master |
| Social cutdown | 9:16, 1080x1920, 60fps (reframed, not cropped) |
| Feed version | 1:1, 1080x1080, 60fps |
| Thumbnail | 16:9, 1920x1080, static PNG from Shot 7A |
| Subtitle file | SRT with all text overlays timed to frame |
| Audio stems | Music, SFX, and combined as separate WAV files |

### Total Runtime Target
**75 seconds** (1:15) for the primary cut. Acceptable range: 65-85 seconds. The pacing notes above should naturally land around 75s. If it runs long, tighten Segment 6 (three audiences) first -- that section can compress to 8 seconds without losing meaning.
