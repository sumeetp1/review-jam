## LinkedIn Launch Post

I'm excited to share something I've been building: Review Jam, a trust-first review marketplace.

Here's the problem we're solving. Brands spend enormous budgets driving traffic to product pages. But when a potential customer lands on that page, they skip the marketing copy entirely. They go straight to the reviews. That moment -- reading reviews, deciding whether to trust them -- is where the purchase decision actually happens.

And that layer is broken. Incentivized five-star reviews. New products with zero social proof. Reviews written on day one that never get updated. Consumers have caught on, and trust in online reviews is eroding fast.

Review Jam approaches this differently. Reviewers earn based on engagement and thoroughness, never based on sentiment. Every review receives a Health Score from 0 to 100, powered by AI bias detection and receipt OCR verification. Reviewers commit to Ownership Journeys, returning to update their reviews at 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year after purchase. Trust tiers from Newcomer to Legend reward long-term credibility.

For brands, we solve the cold start problem with bounty pools, drive conversion with embeddable review widgets, and enable community-driven product discovery. For consumers, the result is reviews you can actually trust.

We're launching on Product Hunt today. If you work in e-commerce, DTC, or product marketing and want to see what honest, verified, long-term reviews can do for conversion, I'd love to connect.
