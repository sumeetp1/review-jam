## Usage Notes

- Replace all `[bracketed]` placeholders before sending.
- Email 1 is best sent Tuesday through Thursday, morning.
- Email 2 should go out 5-7 business days after Email 1 if no response.
- Email 3 works as a standalone outreach or as a third touch after Emails 1 and 2.
- Personalize the subject line or opening line with something specific to the brand (recent product launch, category, known review challenges) for higher open rates.
