# Review Jam - Product Hunt Listing

## Tagline

Reviews sell products. We make reviews trustworthy.

## Short Description

Review Jam is a trust-first review marketplace where reviewers earn for thoroughness, not positivity. Our Health Score (0-100) measures review quality through AI bias detection, receipt verification, and long-term Ownership Journeys. Brands get real reviews. Consumers get real trust.

## Full Description

Every brand spends thousands driving traffic to product pages. But when shoppers arrive, they don't trust your ad copy. They scroll straight to the reviews. The purchase decision happens in that moment, and most review ecosystems are broken: incentivized five-star ratings, zero reviews on new products, and no way to tell real owners from paid shills.

Review Jam fixes the purchase decision layer. Reviewers earn based on engagement and thoroughness, never sentiment. Every review gets a Health Score from 0 to 100, powered by AI bias detection and receipt OCR verification. Reviewers commit to Ownership Journeys, updating their reviews at 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year. This is what honest product feedback actually looks like.

For brands, Review Jam solves the cold start problem through bounty pools, drives conversion with embeddable review widgets, and unlocks community-driven discovery. For consumers, every review is transparent, verified, and built to last. Trust tiers from Newcomer to Legend reward consistency over time, and review forking lets the community build on each other's experiences.

## Maker's First Comment

Hey PH! I built Review Jam because I kept buying products based on reviews that turned out to be garbage. Five stars from accounts that reviewed 40 different products in a week. "Love it!" with no actual detail. We all know the problem.

So I asked: what if reviewers got paid for being thorough instead of being positive? What if you could verify someone actually bought the thing? What if a review updated over time so you knew whether that blender still worked six months later?

That's Review Jam. The Health Score is the core of it -- a 0-100 rating on the review itself, not the product. AI bias detection flags suspicious patterns, receipt OCR confirms real purchases, and Ownership Journeys mean reviews get better with age.

We're launching with bounty pools for brands who want real feedback on new products. If you're a brand tired of the fake review game, or a reviewer who actually cares about helping people make good decisions, I'd love to have you in the community.

Would love your feedback -- what would make you trust a review platform?
